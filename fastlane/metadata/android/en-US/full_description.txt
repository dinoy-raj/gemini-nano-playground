Your own AI playground, running entirely on your phone.

Nano Prompt Ground puts Gemini Nano, the generative AI model built into Android, at your fingertips. Type a prompt, tap the sparkle and watch the answer write itself, token by token. No accounts, no API keys, no cloud. Your words never leave the device.

Under the hood, the app talks to the on-device model through the official ML Kit GenAI Prompt API and Android AICore.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Every prompt is answered locally by Gemini Nano. Questions, notes and drafts stay on your phone, and nothing is sent to a server.

★ WORKS OFFLINE
Once the model is on your device, prompt it on a flight, in airplane mode or anywhere without signal. Inference uses zero mobile data.

★ PROMPT ANYTHING
Summaries, rewrites, ideas, explanations, short stories and haiku. Tap the prompt field to open a full editor, paste from your clipboard in one tap and send.

★ ANSWERS THAT STREAM IN
Watch responses appear as the model writes them, with rich Markdown for lists, headings and code.

★ SEE THE SPEED OF EVERY ANSWER
After each response you get the inference time in milliseconds, the number of output tokens and the finish reason, plus a live seconds counter while the model thinks. A great way to compare prompts and learn how on-device models behave.

★ SHARE TO NANO
Select text in any app, tap Share and pick Nano Prompt Ground. It lands in the prompt field, ready to summarise, translate or rewrite.

★ KNOW YOUR MODEL
Tap the model bar to see which Gemini Nano version runs on your phone.

★ COPY AND CLEAR
Copy any answer with one tap. Clear Nano's cache of recent prompts from a simple dialog whenever you like.

★ EXPRESSIVE BY NATURE
Built entirely with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, with Material 3 Expressive shapes, shared element transitions, playful bounce effects and dynamic colour that follows your wallpaper.

★ OPEN SOURCE, MADE FOR THE CURIOUS
Nano Prompt Ground is open source. Try Android's built-in AI hands-on before adding it to your own app, and see how the Prompt API, the AICore model download and streaming fit together in a real app.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open the app. It checks whether Gemini Nano is available on your device.
2. If the model isn't downloaded yet, the app fetches it through AICore and shows the progress.
3. Start prompting. After the first download, everything runs on device.

SUPPORTED DEVICES
Gemini Nano runs only on phones with AICore and the on-device model, including recent flagship devices such as the Pixel 9 and Pixel 10 series, Samsung Galaxy S26 series and Galaxy Z Fold7, OnePlus 13 and 15, Xiaomi 15 series and other models from Honor, Motorola, OPPO, realme, vivo, iQOO and POCO. Android 13 or later is required. Keep Android AICore and Private Compute Services up to date. On devices without Gemini Nano support, the app lets you know the feature is unavailable.
